⚠ Why Are There Fewer Gaming Companies in P1+P2?
Gaming is not absent from the pipeline — 90% of the 617 gaming apps in our universe have Moloco media cost visibility. The issue is that gaming apps generate lower Moloco media cost than non-gaming apps, because gaming supply primarily flows through Unity Ads, AppLovin MAX, and IronSource rather than Google ADX (which is where non-gaming utility apps are most active).

In P3 (gaming apps with supply visible but below P2 threshold), the Moloco media cost distribution is: p25 = $24/mo · p50 = $120/mo · p75 = $391/mo — our current P2 threshold of $500 filters out 75% of gaming apps.

Methodology — How This Data Is Calculated
Data Sources
  • 📊 dim1_app_v3 — app & company metadata, ST signals
  • 📊 fact_app_basis_v3 — monthly downloads by country
  • 📊 supply_demand_overlap — Moloco media cost, supply visibility
  • 📊 fact_dsp_core — UA spend exclusion
  • 📊 fact_dsp_publisher — publisher exchange SDK signals
  • 📊 download_revenue_v3 — top DL/revenue country
  • 📊 hq_country_triage — GTM-validated HQ country
Inclusion Thresholds
  • ✓ Monthly downloads ≥ 20,000 (latest full month, global)
  • ✓ Primary download market ≠ China / HK / TW
  • ✓ Company HQ in CHN / HKG / TWN / SGP or GTM-verified
  • ✓ Monetization: IAA, IAP, or Hybrid signal present
Exclusion Criteria
  • ✗ App already has Moloco SDK installed
  • ✗ Company has any Moloco UA spend (90d)
P1 / P2 Criteria & Key Fields
  • P1: CHN + media cost >$2K + dl ≥100K + MoM >10%
  • P2: CHN + media cost >$500 + dl ≥50K + MoM >-10%
  • P3: media cost >$0 (incl. all SGP companies)
  • P4: no Moloco media cost signal
Monetization Validation

Sensor Tower's has_ads flag is sometimes inaccurate. We override: if Moloco media cost > $0, the app is confirmed to have IAA inventory → IAP → Hybrid-IAP, Free → IAA.

05 · Understanding Hybrid Types

All three Hybrid variants share has_ads=true AND has_iap=true but differ in app category and recommended Moloco product approach.

Non-Gaming

Hybrid

596
32% of all apps

Non-gaming utility/tool apps (PDF readers, video players, scanners) monetising through both display ads and in-app subscriptions. The advertising side is the primary Moloco SDK opportunity — SDK integration improves fill rate and eCPM on the existing ad inventory.

→ Primary: Moloco SDK (IAA side)
Gaming · Casual

Hybrid-IAA

149
8% of all apps

Casual-genre gaming apps (Casual, Hypercasual, Arcade, Puzzle) with both ad revenue and optional IAP (remove ads, cosmetics). Advertising dominates monetisation despite IAP presence. IAA genre whitelist signals SDK-first approach.

→ Primary: Moloco SDK (ad-first)
Gaming · Mid-core

Hybrid-IAP

247
13% of all apps

Mid-core/hardcore gaming apps (Strategy, RPG, Action) with IAP as primary revenue and supplemental ad placements. IAP drives most revenue, so ROAS-focused UA campaigns are the primary Moloco pitch; SDK integration is secondary.

→ Primary: UA Campaign (ROAS-first)
Combined Hybrid = 992 apps (53%) — the largest single category. This reflects Chinese non-gaming publishers' widespread adoption of dual ad + subscription revenue models. Pitches should address both angles.

11 · Methodology
Data & Methodology

list4_5_6_cold_prospects.sql

Data Sources

  • dim1_app_v3 — app/company metadata, monetization signals (has_ads, has_iap, genre, is_gaming)
  • fact_app_basis_v3 — monthly downloads by country (scale threshold + primary market check)
  • supply_demand_overlap — Moloco supply visibility, media_cost_usd, bid_requests
  • fact_dsp_core — UA spend exclusion (bundle + company level)
  • fact_dsp_publisher — publisher exchange SDK signals
  • download_revenue_v3 — 30d top DL/revenue country
  • hq_country_triage — GTM-validated HQ country override

Inclusion Thresholds

  • Monthly downloads ≥ 20,000 (latest full month, global)
  • Primary download market ≠ CN / HK / TW / MO (overseas-first filter)
  • HQ country in scope: CHN / HKG / TWN / SGP, or GTM-verified
  • Monetization type: IAA, IAP, or any Hybrid variant

Exclusion Criteria

  • Apps with is_moloco_sdk_app = true
  • Apps or companies with Moloco UA spend > 0 in last 90 days

Key Field Definitions

  • moloco_media_cost_usd_30d
    Moloco DSP spend buying this app's supply via open exchange (30-day rolling window from supply_demand_overlap). Not UA spend — this is supply-side cost.
  • has_other_exchange_sdk
    App connected to non-Moloco exchanges (InMobi, Vungle, ADX, etc.) via fact_dsp_publisher.
  • hq_country_source
    GTM_triage = human-verified; ST_original = Sensor Tower fallback, may be inaccurate.
